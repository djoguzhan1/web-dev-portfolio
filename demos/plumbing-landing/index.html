Questions

Things people ask before they call

Do you charge more for nights, weekends or holidays?

No. Same flat-rate price book, 24/7. The only fee that exists is the $79 trip charge if you decline the quote, and that's waived when we do the work.

How fast can you really get here?

Across Phoenix and the East Valley, about an hour is typical, often less. Farther out (Cave Creek, Queen Creek) plan on 60 to 90 minutes. The dispatcher gives you a real ETA on the phone, and your plumber texts a live one.

My water bill jumped. Is it a slab leak?

Maybe. Hot-water lines under post-tension slabs are a common culprit in the Valley. So are running toilets and irrigation leaks. We check the meter first, then use acoustic detection. $299, credited toward the repair if you go with us.

Why do water heaters fail so fast here?

Hard water. Phoenix runs 12 to 18 grains, so sediment builds up and eats the tank from the inside. Flushing yearly helps; a softener helps more. Most tanks here last 8 to 12 years.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Arizona ROC 339112 (CR-37 Plumbing), bonded and insured. Every plumber on our trucks is a W-2 employee and background-checked, and we pull permits when the city requires one.

What's the warranty?

Two years, parts and labor, on every repair. Lifetime on whole-home repipes. If something we fixed fails, we come back and make it right, with no trip fee.
