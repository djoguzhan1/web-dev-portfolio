Questions

Things people ask before they call

How fast can you get here?

Same-day for calls that come in before 2 PM, most days including Saturday. After hours, a live dispatcher (not voicemail) answers the emergency line and sends the on-call tech.

What does a service call cost?

$89 for the diagnostic visit. That fee disappears when you approve the repair. You'll get the repair price in writing, flat-rate, before any work starts, so there's no clock running.

Do you work on my brand?

Yes. Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, American Standard, Amana, Daikin and most others. If a part isn't available same-day, we'll tell you the realistic timeline instead of guessing.

Repair or replace?

The rule of thumb we use: if the repair costs more than a third of a new system and the unit is over 12 years old, replacement usually wins. We'll put both numbers in front of you and let you decide. Nobody works on commission here.

Do you offer financing?

0% APR for 18 months on complete systems, with approved credit through our financing partner. The application takes about five minutes and can be done at the kitchen table.

Are your technicians employees or subcontractors?

Employees. Every technician is drug-tested, background-checked, NATE-certified and drives a marked CoolAir truck. We don't send subcontractors to your home.
