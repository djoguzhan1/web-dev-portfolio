How I work

Simple, and in writing.

No discovery calls you have to book a week out, no account manager in between. You talk to the person doing the work.

  1. Send the brief

    Your business type, what the page or fix needs to do, and any deadline. A link or a few screenshots help. You'll hear back within an hour during my working day.

  2. Fixed quote

    Price, scope and delivery date in writing before anything starts. If the job isn't a good fit, I'll say so instead of taking it.

  3. Build and review

    Landing pages: first draft in 48 hours. Fixes: usually same or next day. You review on your phone and your desktop, and I make the changes.

Questions

Things people ask before hiring.

Can I see your work before hiring you?

Yes. Both demos on this page are live sites, not screenshots. The businesses are fictional, but the code is real and public on GitHub. If you want to see how I'd approach your specific site, send me the link and I'll reply with a short, free assessment of what I'd change and what it would cost.

Why are your prices lower than most?

I'm new on Upwork. Right now, good reviews are worth more to me than margin, so the first clients get introductory prices. The scope and the quality aren't reduced; only the price is. It won't stay this low once the profile has a track record.

Do you build with WordPress or hand-coded?

Both. For one-page landing sites I default to hand-written HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, because it loads faster, breaks less and can be hosted for free. If you need to edit the content yourself, I can deliver the same design as a lightweight WordPress theme; ask for a quote. Fixes are done on your existing WordPress site.

What do you need from me to start?

For a fix: the site link, what's wrong and where you see it, and temporary admin access once we've agreed the job. I'll explain how to create a separate user for me that you delete afterwards.

For a landing page: business name, services, service area, phone number, a few photos if you have them, and any reviews you want shown. I can write the copy from a few messages; you don't need to prepare a document.

Who handles hosting and the domain?

You keep ownership of both. I deploy to your existing host, or set up free hosting on Netlify or GitHub Pages and connect your domain. I'll show you where everything lives so you're never dependent on me to make a change.

What if I don't like the first draft?

You see it early, before most of the polish work, so changes are cheap. Two rounds of revisions are included on landing pages, and I don't call a job finished until it looks right on your phone and your desktop. If something is off, say it plainly; I'd rather fix it than have you accept it.
